# QualiZeal Leave Policy

QualiZeal's leave policy defines the time off available to every employee and how to request it. It applies to all full-time employees across delivery, engineering, sales and corporate functions.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee accrues annual leave each month of service. Annual leave is intended for rest and personal time and should be planned in advance with the reporting manager. Unused annual leave may carry over up to a defined cap; anything above the cap lapses at the end of the leave year.

## Sick leave

Sick leave covers absence due to illness or medical care. Employees should inform their reporting manager as early as possible on the first day of absence. Extended medical absence beyond a short threshold requires a medical certificate.

## Parental leave

Parental leave is available to new parents around the birth or adoption of a child. Eligibility, duration and pay follow the statutory minimum in the employee's country of employment, and QualiZeal may offer more than the statutory minimum where applicable.

## How to apply

Leave is requested through the people system. Submit the request to your reporting manager, who approves or declines based on team coverage. For planned leave, give reasonable notice so delivery commitments are protected.
