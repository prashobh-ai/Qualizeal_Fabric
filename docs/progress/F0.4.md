## Retirement plan

(`surfaces/ask_ui.py`, `surfaces/curator_ui.py`, `surfaces/admin_ui.py`,
`surfaces/dashboard.py`) are retired after F5 — the three static-file
surfaces in `surfaces/static/app/` replace them. Their **data
endpoints** stay in `http_api.py` (that is the recommended reuse in the
new Final Build Instructions §F0.4) so F3–F5 speak to the same API.
