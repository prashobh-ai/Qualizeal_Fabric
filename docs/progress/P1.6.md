## Direction note

The P1.6 spec asks: "`asker.restricted` receives a strict subset of
`asker.public`'s suggestions." The P0.3 role mapping (in this repo)
assigns scopes so that:

- `asker.public` — scopes `["public"]`, base asker who cannot see
  restricted documents;
- `asker.restricted` — scopes `["public", "restricted"]`, an *elevated*
  asker who can also see restricted documents.

Under that mapping, the strict-subset relation flips: `asker.public` is
the strict subset of `asker.restricted`. The invariant the spec is
after (an asker never sees a question their ACL forbids) is the same
either way. Rather than swap the semantic of every existing test
(23 sites), the P1.6 test asserts the subset in the direction the code's
scopes actually give — `asker.public ⊂ asker.restricted`. Recorded here
so a future reader knows why the direction is inverted from the spec
wording. Grounded in the code, not the document (§0.1).

## Deferred

- **Confidence ≥ 0.56 and cited-doc-count ≥ 2 filters.** The spec asks
  for two additional gates whose data source is a per-question rating
  populated by the eval bank runs. The current `question_bank` schema
  stores `(id, tenant, question, expected_docs, family)` but no
  confidence rating. Adding a `question_ratings` companion table (or an
  `confidence`/`co_cited_docs` column) is a schema migration; scope for
  a later phase (P4.3 classifier or P9.1 per-language quality already
  runs the bank and records per-question outcomes — the rating can flow
  from there). The endpoint currently falls back to family-diverse ACL-
  visible questions, which matches leadership's expectation for a demo
  bank of five items.
