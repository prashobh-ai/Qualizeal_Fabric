# L0.4 — Routing sanity for definitions (D3)
Status: DONE

## Files created/changed

- `knowledge_fabric/answer/selector.py`
  - `doc_spread` is now measured over the **reranked top-5**
    (`selected[:5]`), not the whole retrieval set, so a long tail of
    loosely-related documents can no longer escalate a simple question.
  - New **definition rule**: a `what is / who is / define <X>` question
    whose subject `<X>` matches the top document's title resolves to
    **Level 1 Quote it** and locks against document-spread escalation.
    Authority is honoured when a 0-100 score is supplied (wired in
    F2.1); until then a title match on the top document suffices.
  - `multi_document` now raises a lookup **at most to Level 2**; only a
    reasoning/comparison intent takes document spread to Level 3.
  - `classify()` gains optional `doc_titles` and `doc_authority` args.
- `knowledge_fabric/answer/service.py` — builds `doc_titles` for the
  reranked top-5 and passes it to `classify`.
- `eval/route_eval.py` (new) — runs the bank through the full answer
  path, reports the level distribution and the lower-two share, and
  writes `eval/route_eval.md`. F7.5 will expand it (accuracy, escalation
  and refusal rates, cost per level) and point it at the loaded
  `qualizeal` fabric.
- `tests/test_l0_routing.py` (new) — 6 tests: the definition rule →
  Level 1 with the term's document cited first (end-to-end and at the
  selector); a plain multi-document lookup capped at Level 2; a
  comparison still reaching Level 3; doc_spread measured over the top-5.
